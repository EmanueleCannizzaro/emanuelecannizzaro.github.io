# Wikipedia Sandbox Article Instructions

## Overview

This document contains instructions for creating your Wikipedia user sandbox page.

## Files Created

- `wikipedia-sandbox.txt` - The complete wikitext markup for your Wikipedia sandbox article

## How to Use

### Step 1: Access Your Sandbox

1. Go to Wikipedia and log in to your account
2. Navigate to: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Emanuele.cannizzaro/sandbox`
3. Click "Edit" or "Edit source" at the top of the page

### Step 2: Copy the Content

1. Open the file `wikipedia-sandbox.txt` in this repository
2. Copy the entire contents
3. Paste it into the Wikipedia editor
4. Click "Publish changes"

### Step 3: Preview and Refine

1. Click "Show preview" to see how your article will look
2. Review the formatting and content
3. Make any necessary adjustments

## Important Wikipedia Guidelines

### Citations Needed

All statements in the article are currently marked with `{{cn|date=January 2026}}` (citation needed). Before moving this article to mainspace, you should:

1. **Add reliable sources** for each claim
2. **Replace** `{{cn}}` tags with proper citations using the format:
   ```
   <ref>{{cite web |url=URL |title=Title |website=Website |access-date=YYYY-MM-DD}}</ref>
   ```

### Notability Requirements

Wikipedia requires subjects to meet [notability guidelines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability). For engineers and technical professionals, this typically means:

- Significant coverage in reliable, independent sources
- Notable achievements or contributions to the field
- Recognition through awards, patents, or industry impact

### Neutral Point of View (NPOV)

- Avoid promotional language
- Present facts objectively
- Use third-person perspective
- Balance positive and negative aspects (if applicable)

### Content Guidelines

1. **Verifiability**: Every statement should be verifiable through reliable sources
2. **No Original Research**: Only include information from published sources
3. **Biographical Living Persons**: Extra caution for accuracy and neutrality

## Adding Citations

### Example Citation Formats

**For websites:**
```wikitext
<ref>{{cite web |url=https://example.com |title=Article Title |website=Website Name |date=2026-01-24 |access-date=2026-01-24}}</ref>
```

**For news articles:**
```wikitext
<ref>{{cite news |url=https://example.com |title=Article Title |newspaper=Newspaper Name |date=2026-01-24 |access-date=2026-01-24}}</ref>
```

**For journal articles:**
```wikitext
<ref>{{cite journal |last=Smith |first=John |date=2026 |title=Article Title |journal=Journal Name |volume=1 |issue=1 |pages=1–10 |doi=10.1234/example}}</ref>
```

## Before Publishing to Mainspace

If you plan to move this article from your sandbox to Wikipedia mainspace:

1. **Gather reliable sources**: Press releases, news articles, academic publications
2. **Add all citations**: Replace every `{{cn}}` tag with a proper reference
3. **Request feedback**: Use [WikiProject Biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Biography) or [WikiProject Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Engineering)
4. **Consider Articles for Creation (AfC)**: Submit through the AfC process for review

## Conflict of Interest Notice

Since you are writing about yourself, you should:

1. **Declare the conflict of interest** on your user page or the article's talk page
2. **Be extra cautious** about neutrality and verifiability
3. **Consider requesting** someone else to review and improve the article
4. **Use the sandbox** for drafting before moving to mainspace
