'''Emanuele Cannizzaro''' is an Italian-British aerospace engineer, software developer, and data analyst specializing in [[fatigue (material)|fatigue]] and [[damage tolerance]] analysis for aircraft structures. He currently serves as Technical Team Leader at [[Airbus]] in Toulouse, France, where he leads the A350 Nose and Center Fuselage Structural Assembly F&DT Team.{{cn|date=January 2026}}

== Education ==

Cannizzaro earned his Master of Engineering (MEng) degree in Aerospace Engineering from the [[Università degli Studi di Palermo]] in 1998, graduating with the highest honors (110/110 cum laude).{{cn|date=January 2026}} His thesis focused on "A Virtual Subsonic Wind Tunnel Simulator for Educational Purposes." During his studies, he participated in the [[Erasmus Programme]] with an exchange at the [[University of Nottingham]].{{cn|date=January 2026}}

== Career ==

=== Early Career (1999-2004) ===

Cannizzaro began his professional career in 1999 as a Project Engineer at [[Atkins (company)|Atkins]] in Bristol, UK, where he worked on diverse projects including rail crash investigations ([[Hatfield rail crash]], [[Ladbroke Grove rail crash]]) and the [[Beagle 2]] Mars Lander mission.{{cn|date=January 2026}}

From 2001 to 2004, he worked as a Senior CAE Engineer at [[Ricardo plc|Ricardo]] in Leamington Spa, UK, contributing to high-performance vehicle programs including the [[Bugatti Veyron]], [[Ford GT]], and [[Mitsubishi Lancer WRC]].{{cn|date=January 2026}}

=== Aerospace Engineering (2004-Present) ===

Cannizzaro returned to Atkins from 2004 to 2011 as a Senior F&DT Engineer, performing structural analysis for both aerospace and nuclear sectors. He worked on multiple Airbus programs and UK nuclear facilities.{{cn|date=January 2026}}

From 2011 to 2015, he served as Head of F&DT at Assystem UK in Bristol, leading a team supporting various Airbus programs including the [[Airbus A380|A380]] and [[Airbus A400M Atlas|A400M]].{{cn|date=January 2026}}

Between 2015 and 2019, Cannizzaro worked as a Senior F&DT Engineer at Airbus in Bristol, UK, where he held the designation of Airbus Signatory for both F&DT (Fatigue and Damage Tolerance) and FEA ([[Finite Element Analysis]]). During this period, he contributed to the [[Airbus A350 XWB|A350]] and [[Airbus A321|A321LR]] programs.{{cn|date=January 2026}}

Since 2019, he has served as Technical Team Leader at Airbus in Toulouse, France, overseeing fatigue analysis, damage tolerance assessments, and digital transformation initiatives for the A350 program.{{cn|date=January 2026}}

== Technical Contributions ==

=== Digital Twin Technology ===

Cannizzaro has been involved in the development of [[digital twin]] platforms for aerospace applications, including:

* '''Airframe Digital Twin Platform''': A comprehensive system for real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance of airframe structures{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* '''Airframe Fatigue Twin (AF-Twin)''': A specialized digital twin for fatigue life prediction and monitoring of aircraft structures using [[machine learning]] techniques{{cn|date=January 2026}}

=== Software Development ===

He has developed several automation and analysis tools for the aerospace industry:

* '''Time-Series Analytics Studio (TSAS)''': An advanced analytics platform for [[time series]] data analysis with interactive visualization capabilities{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* '''QuASAR''': A digital stress dossier system for automated documentation and analysis of structural assessments{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* '''Aero2Stress''': An automation tool for converting aerodynamic loads to stress analysis inputs{{cn|date=January 2026}}

=== Open Source Contributions ===

Cannizzaro is an active contributor to several [[open-source software]] projects in the scientific computing domain, including:

* [[Armadillo (C++ library)|Armadillo]] - A C++ linear algebra library{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* h5nastran - A HDF5-based format for [[Nastran]] files{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* felyx - Finite element analysis software{{cn|date=January 2026}}
* ferespost - Post-processing tools for finite element analysis{{cn|date=January 2026}}
